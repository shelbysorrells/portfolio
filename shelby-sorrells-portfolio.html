A shared way to measure a communications strategy

Chapter Zero Alliance · Communications Intern · July–September 2026

The problem

The organisation had a new 2026–2027 Communications Strategy and needed a consistent way to benchmark progress against it.

What I did

Built a tracker of 11 KPIs, split across two tabs: Strategy Adoption (is the strategy being put into practice?) and Comms Performance (is the communications work performing?).

What changed

The team has one place to see progress on the strategy, with adoption and performance kept separate so they can tell the two apart.

Finding gaps across a global network

Chapter Zero Alliance · Communications Intern · July–September 2026

The problem

After a rebrand, it was unclear how consistently the new identity showed up across 35+ national chapters, in AI and search results, and on the website.

What I did

  • Audited the branding of 35+ chapters and categorised them by hosting type.
  • Ran an AI visibility audit of how the rebrand appeared across AI and search platforms.
  • Audited the website for accuracy and checked ambassador biographies.

What changed

The AI audit found a 43% post-rebrand name recognition gap, which informed follow-up messaging priorities. The website audit flagged outdated “70 countries” references, and ambassador biographies were corrected.

Running international workshops end to end

Living Imaginaries · Operations and Engagement Coordinator (temporary) · September 2025–March 2026

The problem

Two marine governance workshops brought together 40+ researchers, policymakers, artists and activists across the Netherlands and the UK, and each needed to run smoothly for a mixed group.

What I did

  • Coordinated participants and schedules, prepared materials, and documented each event afterwards.
  • Helped set up internal infrastructure, including payroll systems and a trademark application.
  • Wrote a brand guide covering colours, fonts and imagery.

What changed

Both workshops were delivered with records kept for the team, and the organisation gained operational groundwork it didn’t have before: payroll, a trademark filing and a brand guide.
